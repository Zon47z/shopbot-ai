FAST FASHION

Think Before You Buy!
Fast fashion is a system where clothes are made very quickly and sold at low prices. Brands produce many collections every year, so trends change very fast. As a result, people buy more and more clothes than before.

Why Do People Buy Fast Fashion?
$
Cheap Prices
Fast fashion is affordable and accessible to everyone, making it tempting to buy more.
*
Trendy Styles
New clothes arrive very often, so it is easy to follow the latest fashion trends.
@
Social Media
Seeing influencers on social media, people want to buy new outfits all the time.
Polluting Our Planet
7,000 Litres
One pair of jeans needs about 7,000 litres of water to produce -- that is what one person drinks in 5 years!
Massive Waste
Many clothes are thrown away quickly. Fast fashion uses a lot of water and creates enormous amounts of waste.
The Human Cost

Fast fashion is also affecting workers. Many people are working in difficult conditions and earning very little money. Some workers have worked long hours in unsafe factories.

HOURS Long hours with no breaks
WAGES Extremely low wages
DANGER Unsafe working conditions

Your Choices Matter.
Every time you buy a piece of clothing, you are making a choice. Choose wisely. Choose sustainably. Choose to make a difference.
